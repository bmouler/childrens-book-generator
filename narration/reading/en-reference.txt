REFERENCE VOICE — READ ALOUD ONCE, CALM AND STEADY

Once, at the edge of a quiet forest, there stood a small house with a red door. Every evening, when the sun slipped behind the pines, the lamplighter came walking up the hill. He carried his little ladder on his shoulder, and he hummed as he went. One by one, he lit the lamps along the lane, and one by one, the stars came out above him. The wind settled. The river ran slow and soft. And in the small house with the red door, a kettle began to sing, because bedtime was near.
