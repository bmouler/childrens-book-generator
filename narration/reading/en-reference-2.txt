READ ALOUD: English reference for a second voice, 9 lines
Calm bedtime pace. Speak at normal volume, not a whisper.
Pause 1.5 seconds between lines. Read the whole set twice.
Start the recording, stay silent for 10 seconds, then read.

1. Once, at the edge of a quiet forest, there stood a small house with a red door.

2. Every evening, when the sun slipped behind the pines, the lamplighter came walking up the hill.

3. He carried his little ladder on his shoulder, and he hummed as he went.

4. One by one, he lit the lamps along the lane, and one by one, the stars came out above him.

5. The wind settled. The river ran slow and soft.

6. And in the small house with the red door, a kettle began to sing, because bedtime was near.

7. Goodnight, little one. I'm right here.

8. The moon is up, the stars are out, and everyone is getting sleepy.

9. Close your eyes. Tomorrow the sun will come back.
